Alan Sena · 4LNsz · the mark

Two names for one person, so two marks. The A-quatro goes with the handle, the AS with the name. They are never set side by side; a page may carry one at its head and the other at its foot, as the site does.

On a light ground the brass deepens to #73550f; the form does not change. The drawing is one colour; its word takes the tones the theme sets text in. Ruby is for measurements and never touches the mark.

Clear space

x is a quarter of the mark's height, kept clear on every side. The plate of the icon and the ring of the seal already include it.
